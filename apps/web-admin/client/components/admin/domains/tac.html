What the DNS marks mean

OK
The record is published and has the right value.
Missing
Mesail could not find the record. Create it at your DNS provider.
Wrong
A record exists, but its value is different. Open the domain to compare.
Not checked
Mesail has not checked the record yet. Open the domain and select Check again.
