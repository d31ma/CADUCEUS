When access ends, the device removes the copy (ADR 0006 sections 2 and 4, SPEC AC-OF-9, AC-OF-10, S16, S17). The one-time notice shows how many items were not sent and the first words of each, once (Q13, decided). State 2b shows the number-only variant.

  • “Here are the two logo options. Can you…”

State 3 · Sign out with items waiting (SPEC S17, AC-OF-10)

Sign out and remove your messages from this device?

Signing out removes everything Mesail keeps on this computer, for every domain: messages, drafts, and 1 item that was not sent yet.

You are offline, so it cannot be sent now. Online, Mesail offers to wait for it.

State 6 · “Sign out on all devices” (settings, DELETE /identity/sessions)

Sign out on all devices?

Every device where you are signed in signs out within a minute, this one too. Each one removes its copy of your messages.

Items that a device has not sent yet are not sent. Use this when you lose a device.

Cancel Sign out everywhere

State 7 · “Remove the copy on this device” (settings)

Remove your messages from this device?

Mesail removes the copy of all your domains from this computer (31 MB) and stops keeping one. 4 items that were not sent are removed too.

You stay signed in until you close Mesail. After that, Mesail needs the network.
