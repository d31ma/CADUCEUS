Mesail

The installed apps' lock for the copy on the device (SPEC AC-OF-20, Q22; ADR 0008; TACHYON T15 and T15b: StorageLock, "unlock": "userPresenceWhenAvailable"). Only on macOS, iOS, Windows and Android, and only on a device that keeps a copy. While locked, the app reads and writes nothing: no list, no counts, no queue, no sync. Browsers and the Linux app never show any of this.
