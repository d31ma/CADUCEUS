P4 · In the apps: the words name the system’s own setting

Where to allow the microphone, camera and screen
AppLine under “Mesail cannot use your microphone” (camera and screen the same way)
macOSOpen System Settings > Privacy & Security > Microphone and turn on Mesail. Screen: Screen & System Audio Recording, then quit and open Mesail again.
WindowsOpen Settings > Privacy & security > Microphone and turn on access for Mesail.
iPhone, iPadOpen Settings > Mesail and turn on Microphone.
AndroidOpen Settings > Apps > Mesail > Permissions > Microphone and choose Allow.

The apps show Open settings where the system can open that page; browsers show Check again.
