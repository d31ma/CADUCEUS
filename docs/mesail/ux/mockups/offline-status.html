Mesail

The sync bar sits at the very top of the app, above every pane, on every width (ADR 0006 section 3, SPEC S15, AC-OF-3, AC-OF-7). It shows only when there is something to say. It is one button: 32 px with a mouse, 44 px on touch and under 768 px.

design

design

design

A 2 px line at the bottom of the bar shows the progress. New messages appear in place; nothing jumps while you read.

State 4 · Sending the queue, in the order it was written

design

Items of one conversation go in order; different conversations go side by side.

State 5 · All sent and up to date (hides after 4 seconds)

design

Up to date. 4 sent.

State 6 · Some items need your attention (AC-OF-6)

design

The bar stays until the person opens the list. The sidebar shows “Needs your attention” with a count.

State 7 · Network up, realtime connection down (one wording everywhere, README section 8)

design

Writing still works: items wait and send at the next answer. Pressing the bar tries now.

State 8 · Offline longer than the server keeps changes (AC-OF-15)

design

Each conversation reloads its last 7 days instead of syncing. Items that wait to send are kept and sent after that.
