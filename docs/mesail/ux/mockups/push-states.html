Stop 2 · Where pushes stop, and what the person sees

  • Sign-out

    The browser’s subscription ends. The signed-out page adds: “Notifications in this browser are off.”

  • Removing the copy on this device, or any other wipe

    The subscription ends with the copy. The confirmation adds: “This browser also stops getting notifications.”

  • Removed from a domain

    That domain’s registration is deleted by the server. Nothing extra is shown: the existing “You no longer have access” notice covers it. Other domains keep notifying.

  • 403 access_revoked

    Every subscription of the person ends. The “Your session was ended” screen covers it.
