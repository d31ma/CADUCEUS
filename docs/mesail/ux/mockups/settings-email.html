Messages to colleagues and outside people

Cc people in example.com when an email also goes to them

Colleagues get the message by chat. The email to outside people lists them on Cc, so a reply-all reaches them too. You can change this for each message in the composer.

Example: you write to Bo Lee and carol@partner.org. Bo gets a chat message. Carol gets an email with Bo on Cc.
