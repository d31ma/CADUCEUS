State 2b · Kept browser, right after Sign in: Firefox asks to keep the storage (StorageSpace.persist())

Opening Mesail

Firefox asks if Mesail may store data on this device.

Choose Allow. Then Firefox does not clear your messages when the computer runs low on space.

Firefox’s own prompt opens here (not drawn: it belongs to the browser)

State 2c · The person chose “Block” in Firefox’s prompt

Mesail is open

Mesail still keeps your recent messages here. Firefox may clear them when space runs low; Mesail then loads them again when you are online. Items that wait to send could be lost then.

You can allow it later in Firefox: the lock icon next to the address, then Permissions, then “Store data in persistent storage”.

State 4 · No network, and nothing is kept on this device

You are offline

Sign-in needs the network. Mesail keeps nothing on this device yet, so it cannot open your messages offline.

Connect to Wi-Fi or mobile data. Mesail tries again by itself.

Try again

On a device you own, tick “Keep me signed in on this device” next time. Then Mesail opens without the network.
